# cc-power-link

Shows an energy storage system on a ComputerCraft monitor: a Mekanism induction matrix or a Draconic Evolution
energy core, sent over a wireless/ender modem (including across dimensions). It has its own installer and
configuration tool and does not need cc-mek-scada installed.

Two computers:

- **Sender**: next to the storage, reads it and transmits.
- **Display**: next to an advanced monitor (2x2 or larger), receives and draws the panel.
